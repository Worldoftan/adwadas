BUILD IPA
1. On a Mac with Xcode installed:
   brew install xcodegen
2. cd ios
3. xcodegen generate
4. Open VocabAssistant.xcodeproj in Xcode.
5. Select your Apple developer account/team in Signing & Capabilities.
6. Build the app for your iPhone.
7. For sideloading, archive/export an IPA from Xcode or use your preferred sideload tool.

On Arch Linux, Xcode itself is not available, so a genuine signed iOS IPA cannot be compiled locally.
The M5Stick firmware can be built on Arch with PlatformIO.
